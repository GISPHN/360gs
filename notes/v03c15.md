# v0.3c15 viewer fit rationale

Field observation after v0.3c14:

- `撮影位置から表示` works as intended.
- `全体を表示` can show only the dark clear color.

The reconstruction model is intentionally unchanged from v0.3c13/c14.

The overview fit now uses an opacity-aware visible-Gaussian bounding volume instead of the position distribution of all retained seed Gaussians. It also starts the orbit from a representative observed camera side when available, which is preferable for SH1 appearance to an arbitrary world-space direction.
