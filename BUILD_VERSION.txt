360GS v0.3c23
Gauzilla-inspired browser delivery: SPZ v4 export, viewpoint URLs, and WebGL 2 fallback viewer
Training baseline remains v0.3c22: direct ERP, spherical geometry, post-triangulation pose refinement, SH1 and effective-rank Gaussian regularization are unchanged
Generated 3DGS PLY can be converted locally to SPZ v4 with the vendored @adobe/spz 0.2.2 WebAssembly encoder
The SPZ encoder receives standard 3DGS RDF coordinates and converts them using the official coordinate-system implementation; PLY wxyz quaternions are reordered to SPZ xyzw
The standalone viewer loads PLY or SPZ v4 with locally vendored PlayCanvas 2.21.2 and explicitly selects WebGL 2 so viewing does not require WebGPU
SPZ v4 viewing uses PlayCanvas's external SPZ parser and vendored ZSTD decoder
Viewer state stores yaw, pitch, scene-radius-normalized orbit distance, FOV and optional look position in the URL hash
The generated-result panel gains SPZ v4 download and one-click WebGL viewer controls without changing the c22 Brush runtime
Gauzilla is an architectural reference only; no Gauzilla source is copied
