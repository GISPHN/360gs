360GS v0.3c21
Direct differentiable equirectangular Gaussian projection in Brush/WebGPU
c20 spherical geometry and post-triangulation camera pose refinement are retained unchanged
Six-face cubemap training is replaced by two complementary direct ERP views per source position (native + 180-degree roll)
A spherical-area alpha mask (cos latitude) and seam taper provide weighted photometric supervision while avoiding rectangular-tile seam gradients
Brush adds an Equirectangular camera model with analytic projection Jacobian and analytic covariance/mean VJP; ERP depth sorting uses radial range
Tiny polar caps are excluded from rasterization where longitude is mathematically singular; their spherical area weight is near zero
ERP source width is 1024-2048 px and Brush uses an adaptive 768/1024/1536 px long-edge cap so direct ERP is not unfairly downsampled against the former 512 px per-face cubemap
Complementary seam weights sum exactly to cos(latitude) across the native and 180-degree ERP pair, preventing longitude-dependent double weighting
SH degree 1, c20 trusted geometry, depth guards, Gaussian budget and browser-safe GPU-only growth remain controlled
c20 baseline: 14,747 Gaussians; train 20.47 dB / 0.581; held-out 17.25 dB / 0.530; gap 3.23 dB; anisotropy p90 43.2x
Build date: 2026-08-17
